# Compliance Controls (Starter)

## HIPAA-oriented
- Access control and least privilege
- Audit controls for PHI access
- Transmission security (TLS)
- Data integrity and backup strategy

## GDPR-oriented
- Lawful basis and consent management
- Data minimization and purpose limitation
- Right to access/erasure workflows
- DPIA and records of processing activities

## Shared Security Baseline
- Encryption at rest (KMS-managed keys)
- Secrets management and rotation
- Vulnerability scanning and dependency hygiene
- Incident response playbook
